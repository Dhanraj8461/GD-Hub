Good to know

How do I order?

Choose a package, tap Order now, and enter your profile or post link and WhatsApp number. After you place your order you pay securely with UPI, card or net banking. Your payment is confirmed automatically and your order starts right away.

How do I track my order?

Tap Track order at the top and enter your Order ID. Orders you place on this phone or computer are saved there automatically. Your Order ID is also shown after payment. If you lose it, message us on WhatsApp with the number you used.

Does my account need to be public?

Yes. Keep your account or post public until the order is complete. We never ask for your password.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are completed in 10–15 minutes after payment. Large orders can take longer, and we will tell you on WhatsApp.

Can followers drop?

Some drop is normal on any platform. If you see a drop, message us with your Order ID and we will check it.

Refunds

If we cannot deliver your order, you get a full refund. Once an order has started, it cannot be cancelled.

Is this allowed by the platforms?

Buying engagement goes against the terms of most social platforms, and results can vary. Order only if you accept that risk.
