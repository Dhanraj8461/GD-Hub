How do I order?

Choose a package, tap Order now, and enter your profile or post link and WhatsApp number. Pay securely with UPI, card or net banking. Your payment is confirmed automatically and your order starts right away.

Yes. Keep your account or post public until the order is complete. We never ask for your password.

Most orders are completed in 10–15 minutes after payment. Large orders can take longer, and we will tell you on WhatsApp.
